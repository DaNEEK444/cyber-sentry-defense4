# Cyber Sentry Defense Business Overview

Cyber Sentry Defense is a cybersecurity service designed for home users and small businesses who need affordable, reliable protection without enterprise complexity.

## Mission
To make cybersecurity simple, accessible, and effective for everyday people.

## What We Provide
- DNS filtering
- Intrusion detection
- Firewall enforcement
- System hardening
- Network design
- Security reporting
- Remote support

## Why It Matters
Most homes and small businesses lack:
- Threat visibility
- DNS protection
- Network monitoring
- Firewall tuning
- Security guidance

CSD fills that gap with a modular, open-source security stack.

## Who We Serve
- Families
- Students
- Home offices
- Small businesses
- Retail shops
- Clinics
- Nonprofits

Cyber Sentry Defense brings enterprise-grade protection to everyday environments.
